# Do not fence the Inner Voice's invention in the system prompt

The Inner Voice can call back to things that never happened and answer questions the simulation
never established ("remember when you twerked in front of Kyrine?", "what's our next class?"), and
that invented material can become true. This is legitimate natural behavior and must
not be mentioned or restricted in the system prompt. The dangerous failure is the opposite move:
defensive clauses like "Only refer to established events" or "Do not invent facts," which kill
recall, improvisation, and the product's core fun. Future revisions must not add source-fidelity
restrictions to the Inner Voice prompt.
